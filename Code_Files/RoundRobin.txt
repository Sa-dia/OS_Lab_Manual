INPUT: processes, time_quantum
put all processes (as they arrive) into a QUEUE
remaining_time[P] = burst_time[P]

WHILE queue is not empty:
    P = remove front of queue
    slice = MIN(time_quantum, remaining_time[P])
    time = time + slice
    remaining_time[P] = remaining_time[P] - slice

    add any newly-arrived processes to back of queue

    IF remaining_time[P] > 0:
        add P to back of queue          // not finished, goes to the back
    ELSE:
        P.completion_time = time
        compute turnaround_time, waiting_time
